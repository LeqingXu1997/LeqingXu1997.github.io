Skiing

My Skiing Experience

Skiing represents the perfect escape from the intensity of research work. There's something uniquely refreshing about being in the mountains, surrounded by snow, where the only thing that matters is the next turn.

Favorite Ski Destinations

  • Alpine resorts in Europe - stunning views and well-maintained slopes
  • Local ski areas - convenient for weekend trips
  • Backcountry skiing - for when I want more adventure

What Skiing Teaches Me

  • Balance and precision: Essential skills both on slopes and in research
  • Risk assessment: Knowing when to push limits and when to be cautious
  • Mindfulness: Being present in the moment
  • Perseverance: Getting back up after every fall

Skiing Season

I typically ski from December through March, trying to make at least one longer ski trip each season. These trips serve as important mental breaks that help me return to research with renewed energy and fresh perspectives.

The Connection to My Work

The physics of skiing - understanding trajectories, momentum, and energy - actually relates to some aspects of energy systems research. Plus, the mental clarity gained from time in the mountains often leads to breakthrough insights in my research.
